**Feature level ZF-3491a7**

* [`POST /needs/resolve`](/api/resolve-needs), [`POST /needs/unresolve`](/api/unresolve-needs):
  New endpoints to mark several `mention`, `card_mention` and
  `card_assigned` items of the Perlu kamu inbox done, and to undo that.
  Each item is handled on its own, and the response lists the ids that
  were `done` and the ones that `failed`.
* [`POST /needs/snooze`](/api/snooze-needs), [`POST /needs/unsnooze`](/api/unsnooze-needs):
  New endpoints to hide items of any kind from `GET /needs` until the
  next 07:00 on the workspace's clock, and to bring them back at once.
