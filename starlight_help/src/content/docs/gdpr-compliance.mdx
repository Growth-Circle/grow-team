## Grow Team data handling

Your organization's operator manages the Grow Team installation and its data policies.
Ask that operator about data access, retention, exports, deletion, and backups.
Use the organization's own approved terms and privacy information.

## External services

Cloudflare provides the configured network ingress and email delivery.
AI features require separate review before conversations are sent to a model provider.

The [Zulip mobile push notification service](https://zulip.com/help/mobile-notifications) is an optional upstream service.
It is disabled in the current Grow Team deployment configuration.

[Zulip Cloud](https://zulip.com/zulip-cloud/) and its agreements belong to the upstream provider.
Those agreements do not define the terms for this Grow Team installation.

## Contact your operator

Use [Contact support](/help/contact-support) for questions about your organization's data handling.
