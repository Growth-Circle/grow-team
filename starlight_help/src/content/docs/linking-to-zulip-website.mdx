Use your organization's URL when you invite people to Grow Team.
Use the project repository when you share information about its source code.

## Link to Grow Team

<Steps>
  1. Choose an asset from the [Grow Team logo folder](https://github.com/Growth-Circle/grow-team/tree/grow-team/static/images/grow-team).
  1. Link the image to your organization's URL or the [Grow Team repository](https://github.com/Growth-Circle/grow-team).
  1. Describe Grow Team as a web application for team conversations organized by channels and topics.
</Steps>
