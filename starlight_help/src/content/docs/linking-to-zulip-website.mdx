## Link to sanji

<Steps>
  1. Choose an asset from the [sanji logo folder](https://github.com/Growth-Circle/grow-team/tree/grow-team/static/images/sanji).
  1. Link the image to your organization's URL or the [sanji repository](https://github.com/Growth-Circle/grow-team).
  1. Describe sanji as a web application for team conversations organized by channels and topics.
</Steps>
